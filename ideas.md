# Halcyon Photography Co. — Design Directions

## Three considered approaches

| Theme Name | Very Brief Intro | Probability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aperture Blue | A serene, high-key editorial world that treats every page as a carefully sequenced contact sheet. Cool mineral tones and generous negative space let the photography feel collected rather than decorated. | 0.07 |
| Field Notes | A bright, tactile studio journal with off-kilter captions, paper-like surfaces, and an observational point of view. It is intimate and quietly handmade without becoming rustic. | 0.03 |
| Night Exposure | A cinematic, inky gallery experience with saturated night-sky color and moments of vivid fluorescence. It turns the booking experience into a focused, after-dark ritual. | 0.08 |

## Chosen Direction — Aperture Blue

### Design Movement

The site uses **contemporary editorial art direction with contact-sheet sequencing**, inspired by independent photo books and concise cultural magazines rather than a conventional wedding vendor site. It will feel airy, expertly cropped, and intentionally paced.

### Core Principles

1. **Photography leads, interface supports.** Images are oversized and responsibly cropped; controls remain calm, legible, and secondary.
2. **Asymmetry carries the rhythm.** Content follows offset vertical rails, image stacks, and intermittent full-bleed moments rather than a predictable centered-card grid.
3. **Warmth through language, not palette.** The experience is personable and clear without leaning on familiar warm-cream or terracotta visual shorthand.
4. **Utility can be beautiful.** Booking information is structured like a refined production call sheet: transparent, immediate, and never overcomplicated.

### Color Philosophy

The foundation is **porcelain white and cool mist**, creating the calm, natural light of a north-facing studio. Deep graphite and midnight blue provide dependable contrast and seriousness. **Aerial Blue (#50A6C6)** is the unmistakable Halcyon signature: it appears in confirmations, active states, fine details, and the logo mark, evoking water, sky, and a sharp flash of a kingfisher wing. The palette is intentionally cool and mineral so skin tones in photography remain the visual warmth.

### Layout Paradigm

The site moves along an **unspooling contact strip**. A persistent slim index rail anchors navigation and section labels while photo-led sections shift from left-anchored text to wide visual spills and editorial caption blocks. The booking flow borrows the clarity of a studio call sheet, using a centered task column against an offset detail rail rather than a generic dashboard.

### Signature Elements

1. **Aperture tabs:** small blue semicircle/ticket shapes that mark key section transitions and selected booking states.
2. **Frame codes:** concise image numbering and camera-like metadata appear beneath photography and gallery selections.
3. **Soft blue light leak:** restrained translucent Aerial Blue blurs appear on selected action surfaces, never as a full-screen gradient.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should make clients feel guided rather than processed. Each booking selection resolves into a concrete next fact—an available time, a sunset cue, a retainer amount, or a delivery detail. Hover states reveal editorial captions; decisions receive calm visual confirmation and accessible labels.

### Animation

Page entrances use a single short framing motion: images fade upward by 10px while a blue aperture tab slides into position, staggered 50ms. Booking panels crossfade with a small lateral shift under 220ms. Image hover states gently scale to 1.02 and reveal captions; no looping decorative motion. All animation is disabled or reduced under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System

**Manrope** gives body copy and controls a clean, humane sans-serif voice, while **DM Mono** handles frame codes, time slots, pricing details, and operational labels. Headlines use Manrope in compact, bold weights with deliberate negative tracking; there are no display serifs. The hierarchy prioritizes wide, concise sentences and readable operational information.

### Brand Essence

**Halcyon Photography Co. is a modern photo studio for people who want their meaningful, complicated, beautiful days made effortless to remember.**

Personality: **observant, composed, generous**.

### Brand Voice

Headlines are concise and image-led. Calls to action are specific, never generic, and microcopy anticipates the next question without overselling.

> “Find the light that fits your day.”

> “Put 6:38 PM on hold — we’ll keep it warm for 24 hours.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The wordmark is a tightly tracked, all-caps **HALCYON** paired with a bold abstract mark: a rounded horizontal aperture cut through by a rising wing-like wedge, suggesting both a camera shutter and a kingfisher moving across water. The mark is graphic, single-color, and recognizable at favicon scale—no generated lettering.

### Signature Brand Color

**Aerial Blue — #50A6C6**.

## Style Decisions

- Aerial Blue is a **precision signal** for the logo mark, aperture tabs, active states, frame codes, fine rules, and confirmations—not a dominant full-width background.
- Each major page must show at least one contact-sheet behavior: offset rail, staggered image/text sequence, numbered frame, or editorial caption block.
- Booking and delivery utilities should resemble a refined production call sheet. Mono labels, dividers, selected tabs, and structured facts carry hierarchy instead of soft dashboard cards.
